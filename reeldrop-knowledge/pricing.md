## Plans

- 1 Instagram account, 1 workspace, 1 team member
- Unlimited scheduling & posting
- 3 AI caption generations per month

- Instagram analytics

## Pricing FAQ highlights
- **Free plan**: 1 Instagram account, unlimited scheduling/posting, 5 DM automations (uncapped DM volume), 3 AI captions/month. No credit card needed.
- **How many Instagram accounts can I connect?** Creator supports up to 5, via up to 5 workspaces (1 account per workspace). Starter and Influencer each include 1 workspace / 1 account.
- **MCP access**: Included on Influencer and Creator. Free Starter tier can connect but gets an upgrade prompt instead of tool access.
- **Carousel image allowance**: Every carousel slide, cover, edit, and AI reel thumbnail generated costs one of the Creator plan's 100 monthly images, pooled across every workspace. Extra images purchasable as credits (no expiry).
- **Safety**: ReelDrop publishes through the official Instagram Graph API as a Meta Tech Provider. It never asks for your Instagram password.
- **Billing**: Prices in USD (Canadian customers billed in USD, not CAD); India billed in INR at the rupee prices above. Plans are month-to-month, no contract — upgrade/downgrade/cancel anytime from billing settings.
- **Company**: ReelDrop is owned and operated by 10517933 Canada Ltd., based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
